Large Language Models (LLMs) are deep learning systems trained on massive text corpora using transformer architectures. They learn statistical patterns in language through self-supervised pretraining on billions of tokens. Key capabilities include text generation, question answering, summarization and code completion. Prominent LLMs include GPT-4, Claude, Llama and Gemini. LLMs are evaluated on benchmarks such as MMLU, HumanEval and HellaSwag. Fine-tuning techniques like RLHF and LoRA adapt pretrained models to specific tasks or safety requirements.
